The people behind Gulsabi

Meet the Team

Gulsabi is being built by a focused multidisciplinary team bringing together creative vision, business, child development, technology and growth.

Leadership & Product

Deepak Thomas

Founder & Managing Director, Pixnut Media Private Limited

With a decade of experience across brand building, creative direction, e-commerce and business operations, Deepak leads Gulsabi’s product vision, original IP, brand, content strategy, go-to-market and overall execution.

Operations & Finance

Avinash Vattapparakkal

Co-founder & Director, Operations & Finance

With a decade of experience across fintech and wealth-management operations, Avinash leads Gulsabi’s operations, finance and business processes, helping build the discipline and structure required to scale the company.

Child Development

Chinchu Mangalathil

Psychologist

With experience in psychology and child development, Chinchu guides Gulsabi on age-appropriate experiences, learning validation and how young children engage with the product.

Technology

Ashith Wilson

Technology Advisor

With experience in large-scale technology infrastructure at Cloudera and previously Qubole, Ashith advises Gulsabi on technical architecture, engineering decisions, reliability and scalability as the platform grows.

Business & Growth

Swaroop Mohan

Head, Strategy & Partnerships

An entrepreneur with experience building consumer businesses and co-founding Wildermart, Swaroop leads strategy and partnerships for Gulsabi, supporting business development, ecosystem relationships, commercial opportunities and the platform’s growth strategy.
